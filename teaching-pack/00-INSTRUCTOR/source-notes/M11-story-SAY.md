Other tiles can be approve-worthy so the form is not a wipeout. Diane Halvorsen, PE’s skepticism is justified: the county would have seen an undefined score and a recycled false trend.

### Teach 25

- Dashboard and metrics: a tile is only as good as its definition, units, source, and timestamp.
- Time series on a dashboard still need the same honesty as a lab plot—a red arrow is a claim, not a fact.
- Agent-built report + agent autonomy: FOREMAN can assemble overnight; that does not license publish.
- Human review checkpoint: for each tile, **approve / fix / pull** with a written reason. Students practice being that checkpoint.
- Canon names: Diane Halvorsen, PE; Wes Tanaka, EIT; Otter Bend; FOREMAN (orange = machine).
- Save the two designed errors for Reveal. Do not teach “the score is 87 and undefined” as a slide bullet before the hunt.
