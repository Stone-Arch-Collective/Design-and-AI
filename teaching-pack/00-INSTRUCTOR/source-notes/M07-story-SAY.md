# SEIS 201 M07 — It does not know what it does not know

Canon-revised source SAY. Cloud hard-review pass. Stored instructor-side; not a student handout.

## Story spine

1. The team receives the FOREMAN damage report for Otter Bend Lift Bridge. It flags no significant defects with high confidence and attaches a one-page training summary.
2. Students review 12 photos. Diane Halvorsen, PE trusts the speed; Wes Tanaka, EIT begins a memo accepting the rating.
3. Students discover training consists almost entirely of highway girder bridges from warm states, with no movable-bridge examples.
4. Students identify absent Otter Bend conditions: lift machinery, freeze and thaw cycles, and road salt exposure.
5. FOREMAN rates machinery-room photos with the same confidence as girder photos despite no relevant training coverage and shows no uncertainty.
6. Wes polishes the accept-memo; Diane skims and passes it. This is liability color seeded inside M13’s existing messy unit-mismatch handoff—a burn on Wes, not students. It does not rewrite M13 as “the M07 memo returns.”
7. A student defends refusing the tool based on representativeness and distribution shift. Diane remains in BELIEVER mode.
8. Students submit Note version 2 only. The close lightly hooks seasonal change without naming temperature-as-damage.

### Lab

- Compare the 12 photos against the training-summary distribution.
- Mark photos where site context differs from training.
- Observe that confidence remains high despite domain shift.
- Discuss why the model fails to express uncertainty on out-of-domain inputs.
