## Story Spine (6-8 beats)

1. Kinnick County needs a **same-day** decision on an overweight load permit for a **night crossing** at Otter Bend.
2. FOREMAN (orange) recommends the crossing is fine and attaches one chart.
3. Diane Halvorsen, PE is on a site visit and **cannot be reached**. Students own the decision.
4. Wes Tanaka, EIT is thin or absent — not the decider and not a co-solver.
5. Students get **one clean exam file** (same for everyone), built from data shapes they already worked in Units 1–2, plus FOREMAN’s recommendation and chart.
6. In 75 minutes they must check a claim, describe strain with a spread, scale test-truck strain to the permit load with an interval, name one chart flaw, and answer **yes / yes with conditions / no** with note version 2.
7. Grading rewards reasoning and the record — not which branch they pick. **Yes with conditions** and **no** are both defensible.
8. Collect papers. No Unit 3 spoilers.

## Exam call (student-facing)

Kinnick County Public Works needs a decision **today** on an overweight permit for a night crossing at the Otter Bend Lift Bridge. FOREMAN recommends approval and says the crossing is fine. Diane Halvorsen, PE is on a site visit and cannot be reached. You have the exam file, FOREMAN’s recommendation (orange), and one chart. You are the human review checkpoint.

## Student work (75 minutes)

Complete all of the following in the exam booklet / note version 2:

1. **Check one claim:** Pick one claim in FOREMAN’s recommendation and check it against the source data in the exam file. Show the check.
2. **Spread, not just a mean:** Describe the relevant strain data with a spread (range, IQR, or interval — not a lone mean).
3. **Scale with an interval:** Scale the test-truck strain to the permit load by ratio, and report an interval (not a single point as if exact).
4. **Chart flaw:** Name **one** flaw in FOREMAN’s chart. Explain why it matters for a county reader.
5. **Decision + note v2:** Answer **yes**, **yes with conditions**, or **no**. Write note version 2 that a PE could skim: what you checked, what you still do not know, and why your decision follows.

Permit tonnage, gauge IDs, and exact file tables are in the CB exam asset (placeholders on the student prompt — do not invent numbers in the SAY).
