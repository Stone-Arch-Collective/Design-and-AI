## Instructor reveal

FOREMAN’s designed error is false precision: eight significant figures and no range when the
underlying fatigue data support only two or three significant figures. The class names sampling
variability in the repeated random samples and distinguishes the finite demonstrator from bridge
evidence. The actual Otter Bend core sample is useful for its observed shoulder locations but
does not establish whole-deck representation.
