Run of day · 75 minutes

00–10 · Inbox

The public-release request arrives.

SLIDE

Student 1–3 — title, publish request, and five-tile board.

SAY · CANON

FOREMAN: “County-facing Otter Bend health dashboard assembled overnight. Five tiles. Requesting approval to publish to the public portal. No human review requested. Confidence high.” Diane Halvorsen, PE: “The county will see this. I am not rubber-stamping a pretty board.” Wes Tanaka, EIT: “I barely slept. I’m not walking the tiles with you—use the form.”

HAND OUT

FOREMAN-M11 dashboard stub and H11-01. Keep the reveal and key closed.

10–35 · Teach

Defined metrics, time-series claims, autonomy, and review authority.

SLIDE

Student 4–9 — five concepts and the checkpoint vocabulary.

SAY · CANON

A tile is only as good as its definition, units, source, and timestamp. A red arrow is a claim, not a fact. FOREMAN can assemble overnight; that does not license publish. For every tile, record approve, fix, or pull with a written reason.

WATCH FOR

Do not teach which two tiles fail. Do not imply all automation or all dashboard tiles are untrustworthy.

35–65 · Lab

Walk all five tiles, then make the board-level call.

SLIDE

Student 10–12 — tile walk and publish decision.

DO

For each tile: check definition, units, source, timestamp, and whether the time-series story matches earlier evidence. Require one decision and one sentence per tile.

COMMIT

Students complete all five rows and ALLOW/HOLD before discussion. Wes does not supply answers.

DEBRIEF

Only after forms are complete: which survived, which need a fix or pull, and why?

65–70 · Note

Create the audit trail.

SLIDE

Student 13 — Note version 2.

SAY · CANON

Hand in the completed review form plus Note version 2: what was approved, fixed, or pulled, and why Diane Halvorsen, PE should or should not allow publish.

70–75 · Reveal + close

Identify the two failures and retain human ownership.

SLIDE

Reveal 1–8, then Student 15.

SAY · CANON

Reveal the undefined 87/100 score and the raw M08 trend recycled with a red “worsening” arrow. The other three tiles can be approve-worthy. Undefined aggregate scores and recycled bad trends do not ship to the county. Publish is a human decision.

BOUNDARY

Leave open that chart craft still matters without naming later chart tricks.

Close M11
