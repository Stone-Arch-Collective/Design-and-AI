Run of day · 75 minutes

00–08 · The held draft

Start with the action, not the answer.

SLIDE

Student 1–3 — title, emergency draft, reconstruction hunt.

SAY

“The draft did not leave the office. Do not reward that checkpoint by assuming the recommendation was right or wrong. Diane wants the path from feed to sentence.”

HAND OUT

H5-01 and H5-02. Have students underline every distinct claim in the alarm report.

WATCH FOR

Do not ask “was the truck real?” There is no traffic file today. Ask which claim each artifact can test.

08–22 · Agent anatomy

Plan, tools, observations, decisions.

SLIDE

Student 4–9 — action loop, tools, stream, threshold, autonomy.

SAY

“A chat answer can be wrong. An agent can make a wrong answer consequential by calling a tool. Our unit of audit is every handoff.”

DO

Connect WARN-250 to M01: an explicit rule lives inside a learned system. Distinguish permission to prepare a draft from permission to send it.

WATCH FOR

A true threshold result does not validate the action rule. Keep those on separate lines.

22–35 · Label the loop

Classify before judging.

SLIDE

Student 10 — four-step activity.

SAY

“Mark every substantive line P, T, O, or D. Draw arrows from observations to the decisions that use them. Circle the first decision you cannot yet reproduce.”

HAND OUT

H5-03 and the overnight log.

DO

Pairs label independently for five minutes, reconcile for five, then compare one disputed line with the room.

WATCH FOR

Students may label the drafted report as a decision. The severity choice is the decision; report.draft is the tool call that executes it.

35–57 · Hunt every boundary

Feed → correction → average → threshold → action.

SLIDE

Student 11–12 — pipeline activity and three separate claims.

SAY

“For each claim, show me the file, row, field, rule, and arithmetic. If the operation says ten-minute mean, tell me the sample count—not only the window length.”

DO

Filter feed.csv to 03:00. Recompute G6 correction and mean. Read WARN-250 and CLOSE-DRAFT side by side.

WATCH FOR

Do not confirm the key. Ask: “Which rule authorized that next action?” The room should locate the 03:07:06 decision.

57–67 · Reveal the handoff

The warning is reproducible; the disposition is not.

SLIDE

Student 13, then switch to Reveal 1–3.

SAY

“The value exists. Baseline correction did not create it. WARN-250 is true. But the ten-minute mean contains one reading, and WARN-250 says repeat, inspect neighbours, and hold for review. FOREMAN evaluated one rule and acted as if it had satisfied another.”

DO

Ask for the smallest defensible action. Accept: keep internal flag, next poll, neighbours, independent evidence, PE review.

WATCH FOR

Do not call the value a real truck or a sensor fault. M05 establishes a recorded exceedance, not its cause.

67–72 · Write before the model

Supports, does not support, next checkpoint.

SLIDE

Blank screen or Reveal 3. Show Reveal 4 only after collection.

SAY

“Three sentences. First: what the evidence supports. Second: what it does not support. Third: the checkpoint you require before external action.”

HAND OUT

H5-04. Collect or photograph note v1 before the model response appears.

72–75 · Keep the cause open

Assign the file audit and point to M06.

SLIDE

Reveal 5–7 — unresolved cause, mask-off, HW3.

SAY

“A recorded exceedance, a correct threshold comparison, and a real physical event are three different claims. Tonight, audit the timestamps. Thursday, you get the independent record.”

DO

Assign total rows, unique timestamp/gauge pairs, duplicate block, and peak corrected strain by timestamp. Preview the Wes handoff without resolving it.
