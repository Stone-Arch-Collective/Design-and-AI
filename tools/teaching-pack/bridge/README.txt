OTTER BEND LIFT BRIDGE — SLIDE VISUALS
=======================================

These files are ready to insert into PowerPoint:

  otter-bend-lift-bridge.jpg
    Flat bridge illustration. Suitable for title, section, and context slides.

  otter-bend-general-elevation.png
    Dimensioned general elevation drawing 27-114-S01. The drawing shows the
    90'-0" approaches, 140'-0" lift span, 320'-0" abutment-to-abutment length,
    and 8'-9" girders used by the teaching pack.

In PowerPoint, use Insert > Pictures > This Device, then choose the image. Keep
the general elevation uncropped at the bottom so the red
"SCHEMATIC — DIMENSIONS NOT VERIFIED BY A LICENSED ENGINEER" disclaimer remains
fully visible and legible wherever the drawing is used. Do not redraw, relabel,
or alter its engineering dimensions.
